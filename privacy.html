Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Video Fabrikasi is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube and Instagram accounts. No data is collected from, sold to, or shared with third parties.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed: the owner's own channel information, video upload status, public statistics of the owner's own videos, comments on the owner's own videos (to post replies the owner personally approves), the owner's own playlists, and public search results (titles, durations and topics of public videos, used only to analyze trending topics). It is used only to upload and schedule videos, organize them into playlists, reply to comments, and measure performance.

Storage and deletion

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's private server. Any YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, except aggregated public statistics permitted by YouTube policies. To delete all stored data, contact kadirdemirkir@gmail.com; it will be deleted within 7 days.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
